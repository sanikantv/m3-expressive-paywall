# Storylines

## A. One checklist, kept (picked)
Device: a list of Google Play rules travels through the film; form: object-to-object chain.
The film opens on the real demo app ("your app is ready") and names the missing piece. Play's four
disclosure rules appear as a checklist on a dark sheet. The library's mark lands, six real paywalls
roll past on one phone, the 20-line quick start collapses into the phone, and the camera punches into
the real footer where the same four rules now sit, checked. Billing swaps inside one lambda; the
phone flips light, dark, dynamic; the mark carries the end card.
Beats: hook → rules → reveal → six designs → code → disclosure (payoff) → billing → theme → end card.
Look: Material 3 surfaces of the app itself (lavender #FEF7FF / #EADDFF, dark #1D1B20), Google Sans
Flex + Google Sans Code, MaterialShapes cookies, real screenshots at ~2x.
Sound: Liam voiceover is the clock; whoosh on world changes, pops on checklist items, one bass impact
on the reveal, key ticks on code. No music source available on this machine.

## B. Before and after, one app
Device: life before and after; form: split screen to one. A plain, ruleless upgrade screen on the left
churns through review rejections while the right side calmly builds the expressive one.
Not picked: it needs an invented "bad" paywall and a mocked rejection screen.

## C. The paywall that morphs
Device: the feature as the hook; form: match cuts on one shape. The selection cookie stays fixed in
frame while everything around it changes: radio, spotlight card, timeline node, app icon.
Not picked: striking, but tells the shape API more than the developer's task.
