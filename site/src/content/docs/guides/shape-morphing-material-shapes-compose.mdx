  - q: Which shapes are available?
    a: MaterialShapes includes Circle, Square, Pill, Cookie4Sided to Cookie12Sided, Sunny, VerySunny, Flower, Clover4Leaf, Clover8Leaf, Burst, SoftBurst, Heart, Gem and more. They are RoundedPolygon values from androidx.graphics.shapes.
  - q: Do I need the graphics-shapes dependency?
    a: material3 brings it transitively. Add androidx.graphics:graphics-shapes explicitly if you use Morph or RoundedPolygon directly, as this guide does.
  - q: Why does my morph restart on every recomposition?
    a: The Morph or its polygons are created again on each recomposition. Wrap the Morph in remember, and keep target shapes as stable instances.

**Short answer:** call `.toShape()` on any `MaterialShapes` polygon to use it as a normal Compose
`Shape`. To animate between two shapes, create a `Morph(start, end)`, wrap it in a small `Shape`
that calls `morph.toPath(progress)`, and animate `progress` with the theme's motion scheme.

## In the paywalls

M3 Expressive Paywall uses this in two places: the selection radio morphs from a circle into
`Cookie9Sided`, and the [plan picker](/components/plan-picker-paywall/) spotlight card morphs
between a cookie, a sunny shape and a flower as you switch plans.

## Tips

- Morph between shapes with similar complexity. Circle to cookie looks smooth; circle to heart
  can twist.
- Rotate the morphed shape slightly with `graphicsLayer { rotationZ = … }` for more motion.
- Respect reduced motion: with `MotionScheme.standard()` the springs are calmer.
