**Short answer:** show the trial as three steps: today (full access), a reminder a couple of days
before the end, and the day billing starts with the exact price. Put the trial terms in the
disclosure under the button, and make the button say "Start free trial" only when the selected
plan really has one.

## Why a timeline works

A free trial asks the user to trust you with a future charge. Showing the reminder and the
billing day turns a vague risk into a clear plan. It is also what Google Play's policy asks for:
the trial length, when it converts, the price after it and how to cancel, before signup. See
[the requirements](/guides/google-play-subscription-paywall-requirements/).

## With plain Compose

A timeline is a `Column` of rows. Each row has an icon node with a vertical line under it, and
text to the right. Give the row `Modifier.height(IntrinsicSize.Min)` so the line can fill the
height of the text with `Modifier.weight(1f)`.

## Tips

- Keep the reminder honest: if the timeline says Day 5, send it on day 5.
- Use the same trial length everywhere: Play Console, `trialText` and the timeline.
- Pair the trial plan with a plan that has no trial, so users can pay right away if they prefer.
