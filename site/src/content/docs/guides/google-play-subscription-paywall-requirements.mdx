**Short answer:** Google Play wants the subscription's terms to be clear where the user decides:
the cost, how often they are billed, that it renews automatically, the details of any free
trial or intro offer, and how to cancel. Put them next to the purchase button, with no extra
taps needed to see them.

<Aside type="caution">
  This page summarizes Google's
  [Subscriptions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533)
  as checked on 2026-10-26. It is not legal advice. Read the policy itself before you ship, as
  it changes.
</Aside>

## What the policy asks for

According to Google's Play Console Help page on subscriptions, your app must clearly disclose:

- [ ] **The offer terms**: what the subscription includes.
- [ ] **The cost**.
- [ ] **The billing frequency**, such as per month or per year.
- [ ] **That it auto-renews**.
- [ ] **Whether a subscription is needed to use the app**: what works without one.

These belong in promotions, splash screens and plan-selection screens, without the user taking
extra steps to find them.

For **free trials and intro offers**, before the user signs up:

- [ ] How long the trial or intro price lasts and what it includes
- [ ] When and how it converts to a paid subscription
- [ ] The price after the trial
- [ ] How to cancel before it converts

And users need an **easy way to manage or cancel** the subscription, for example a link to the
Google Play subscriptions page.

## Patterns Google lists as violations

- Monthly plans that don't say they renew automatically
- Annual plans shown mainly as a monthly price
- Terms that are only partly translated
- Hiding what is available without a subscription
- Calling an auto-renewing plan a "Free Trial" product
- Multi-step flows that lead to accidental subscriptions
- No clear statement of when a trial becomes paid
- No way to cancel

## In Compose

A disclosure line under the button covers price, period, trial and renewal. This plain
material3 version shows the idea:

<Snippet file="FromScratch.kt" region="scratch-paywall" />

M3 Expressive Paywall builds the same line from each `PaywallPlan` and puts it in a shared footer
on every design, together with Restore, Terms and Privacy links:

- trial plan: "7-day free trial, then $29.99 per year. Renews automatically until canceled. Cancel anytime in Google Play."
- one-time plan: "$79.99 one-time payment. No subscription, no renewals."

What the library does **not** do for you:

- It can't know what your app offers without a subscription. Say it in your copy, or use the
  [Free vs Pro table](/components/comparison-paywall/).
- It doesn't add a "Manage subscription" link inside your app. Add one in your settings screen
  that opens `https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions`.
- Your trial text must match the trial configured in Play Console.
