faq:
  - q: Does it work with stable material3?
    a: Not with 1.4. The library is built on androidx.compose.material3 1.5.0-beta02, the first release line where the Material 3 Expressive components it uses (ButtonGroup, ToggleButton, MaterialShapes, wavy progress) are available. It brings that version with it.
  - q: What is the minimum SDK?
    a: minSdk 26 (Android 8.0). Your app must compile against SDK 37 because material3 1.5.0-beta02 requires it; targetSdk can stay lower.
  - q: Does it support dark mode and dynamic color?
    a: Yes. Every color comes from MaterialTheme.colorScheme, so the paywalls follow your theme, including dynamic (wallpaper) colors on Android 12 and newer.
  - q: Do I need RevenueCat or another billing SDK?
    a: No. The library has no billing code. Use Google Play Billing directly, RevenueCat, or anything else that gives you prices and a purchase function.
  - q: Can I use it with Compose Multiplatform?
    a: Not today. It is an Android library and uses Android resources for its strings.
  - q: How do I change the animation speed?
    a: The paywalls use MaterialTheme.motionScheme. MotionScheme.expressive() gives springy motion, MotionScheme.standard() calmer motion. Set it in MaterialExpressiveTheme.
  - q: Can I translate the text?
    a: Yes. Every default string is in the library's strings.xml with a pw_ prefix, so you can override them in your app's values-xx folders, or pass title and subtitle parameters directly.
  - q: Is it free for commercial apps?
    a: Yes. It is MIT licensed, so you can use and modify it in commercial apps. Keep the license notice.
  - q: Does it handle Google Play policy for me?
    a: It shows the price, billing period, trial terms and auto-renew text for the selected plan, plus Restore, Terms and Privacy links. You are still responsible for your final copy and prices, and for a way to manage the subscription.
  - q: Why do I need OptIn for LimitedOfferSheet?
    a: Its sheetState parameter uses material3's SheetState, which material3 marks ExperimentalMaterial3Api. Add OptIn(ExperimentalMaterial3Api::class) where you call it.
