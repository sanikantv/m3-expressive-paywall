`ComparisonPaywall` is design 2 of [M3 Expressive Paywall](/). Shows what the free tier includes and what Pro adds, as a table with a highlighted Pro column. Text values such as "50" vs "Unlimited" replace the icons where a limit is easier to read than a check mark.

## When to use it

- Your free tier is generous and users ask "what do I actually get?"
- You want to show what is available without a subscription, which Google Play's policy expects.

## Customization

Rename the columns with `freeLabel` and `proLabel` ("Basic" and "Plus" above). The Pro column uses `primaryContainer`, so it follows your theme.

## Accessibility

- Check and cross icons have content descriptions ("Included", "Not included"), so the table reads correctly with TalkBack.
- Plan tiles are radio options, like the plan cards in the other designs.

## Common mistakes

- Too many rows. Six to eight rows keep the plans above the fold on most phones.
- More than three plans: the tiles get narrow. Use the classic design for four or more.
