Six paywall screens, one shared contract: a list of `PaywallPlan`s in, `onPurchase`, `onRestore`
and `onClose` out. Pick by what you sell and where the paywall appears.

| Design | Best for | Expressive APIs |
|---|---|---|
| [Classic plans](/components/classic-plans-paywall/) | Two or three plans, the safe default | `MaterialShapes`, `Morph`, `MotionScheme` |
| [Free vs Pro table](/components/comparison-paywall/) | A generous free tier | Emphasized type, morphing plan tiles |
| [Trial timeline](/components/trial-timeline-paywall/) | Free trials | `MaterialShapes` timeline nodes |
| [Plan picker](/components/plan-picker-paywall/) | Two to four short-named plans | Connected `ToggleButton` group, `Morph` |
| [Limited-time offer](/components/limited-offer-sheet/) | Win-back and launch discounts | `LinearWavyProgressIndicator`, `ModalBottomSheet` |
| [Onboarding end](/components/onboarding-paywall/) | The last onboarding step | `HorizontalPager`, emphasized type |
