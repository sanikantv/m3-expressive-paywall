`LimitedOfferSheet` is design 5 of [M3 Expressive Paywall](/). A `ModalBottomSheet` you can show over any screen: a big discount, the original price struck through, a countdown and a wavy progress bar that drains as time runs out. When the time is up, the button switches to "Offer ended" and is disabled.

## When to use it

- Win-back offers for lapsed subscribers.
- A launch or seasonal discount with a real deadline.

## Usage

<Aside type="note" title="Opt-in needed">
  `LimitedOfferSheet` exposes material3's `SheetState`, which material3 marks
  `@ExperimentalMaterial3Api`. Add `@OptIn(ExperimentalMaterial3Api::class)` where you call it,
  as in the example.
</Aside>

## Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `plan` | `PaywallPlan` | required | The discounted plan. Its `price` is the offer price. |
| `originalPrice` | `String` | required | Regular price, shown struck through. |
| `discountLabel` | `String` | required | Short text in the badge, such as "-50%". |
| `offerEndsAtMillis` | `Long` | required | When the offer ends, as `System.currentTimeMillis()` time. |
| `legal` | `PaywallLegal` | required | Terms and Privacy URLs for the footer links. |
| `onPurchase` | `(PaywallPlan) -> Unit` | required | Called with the selected plan when the button is tapped. Start your billing flow here. |
| `onRestore` | `() -> Unit` | required | Called from the Restore link. |
| `onClose` | `() -> Unit` | required | Called from the close button. |
| `modifier` | `Modifier` | `Modifier` | Applied to the sheet. |
| `title` | `String` | "Limited-time offer" | Headline. |
| `subtitle` | `String` | "A special price, just for you." | Line under the headline. |
| `offerDurationMillis` | `Long` | `15 * 60_000L` | Full length of the offer. Scales the progress bar. |
| `sheetState` | `SheetState` | Hidden, skips the half-expanded state | Sheet state, if you need to control it. |


Full signature: [LimitedOfferSheet in the API reference](/api/m3-expressive-paywall/dev.sanikant.m3.paywall/-limited-offer-sheet.html).

## Customization

Intro prices need the full story: put "First year only, then $29.99 per year" in `priceDetail`. `onClose` is called both by the close button and by swiping the sheet down.

## Accessibility

- The countdown is announced as one phrase ("Offer ends in 12 min 30 sec") instead of separate digits.
- The sheet has a close button in addition to swipe-to-dismiss.

## Common mistakes

- Resetting the deadline on every launch. Fake urgency breaks consumer protection rules; store the real end time.
- Leaving out the regular price after the intro period.
