## v1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

First release.

- Six paywalls: `ClassicPlansPaywall`, `ComparisonPaywall`, `TrialTimelinePaywall`,
  `PlanPickerPaywall`, `LimitedOfferSheet`, `OnboardingPaywall`.
- Shared Google Play compliance footer: price, period, trial terms, auto-renew line, Restore,
  Terms and Privacy.
- Built on `androidx.compose.material3:material3:1.5.0-beta02`, minSdk 26.
- Demo app set in Google Sans Flex, with gallery, light/dark/system theme, dynamic color and
  motion toggles, predictive back.

Install: `com.github.sanikantv:m3-expressive-paywall:v1.0.0`
