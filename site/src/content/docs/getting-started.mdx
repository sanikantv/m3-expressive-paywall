This page takes you from an empty Compose screen to a working paywall. Plan on five minutes.

## Requirements

| | Required |
|---|---|
| minSdk | 26 (Android 8.0) |
| compileSdk | 37 |
| `androidx.compose.material3` | 1.5.0-beta02 or newer (comes with the library) |
| Kotlin | 2.2 or newer (the library is built with 2.2.10) |

<Aside type="caution" title="Why compileSdk 37">
  material3 `1.5.0-beta02` is the first release with the Expressive components the paywalls use,
  and its artifacts require `compileSdk = 37`. Your `targetSdk` can stay where it is.
</Aside>

## Your first paywall

<Snippet file="QuickStart.kt" region="quick-start" title="UpgradeScreen.kt" />

What each part does:

- **`plans`**: what the user can buy. `price` and `period` are display strings, so pass the
  localized price your billing SDK gives you. The plan with a `badge` is preselected.
- **`features`**: the benefits list. Icons come from `PaywallIcons`, or pass any `ImageVector`.
- **`legal`**: your Terms and Privacy URLs. They open in the browser from the footer.
- **`onPurchase`**: gets the selected `PaywallPlan`. Start your billing flow here.
- **`onRestore`** and **`onClose`**: the Restore link and the close button.

Under the button the paywall writes the disclosure line for the selected plan, for example
"7-day free trial, then $29.99 per year. Renews automatically until canceled. Cancel anytime in
Google Play."
